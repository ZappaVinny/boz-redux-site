Make Zombies yours.

Build your own maps, make your own weapons and rewrite the rules, all with free tools. Or just grab what the community has made and play.

Browse verified mods ↓ Start modding ↓

What you can make

Every model, machine, door and trap from every map is ready to drag in. Perk machines, the mystery box and Pack-a-Punch work as soon as you place them.

Maps

Start from an empty floor, or remix Kino der Toten, Ascension or Call of the Dead. One click puts you in your map to play it.

Weapons

Copy any gun, change its damage, fire rate and ammo, give it your own model, and edit its animations and Pack-a-Punch upgrade.

Rules

Shape every round: how many zombies, how tough and fast they are. Set dog rounds, points, power-ups and the box.

Models and sounds

Bring in your own models and textures, replace any of the game's sounds, and give each weapon its own.

Scripts

Ready to go further? Lua scripts let you make buttons, traps, teleporters and easter eggs, and change how the game plays.

Co-op and sharing

Every map works in LAN co-op, and one click packages your work as a mod that other players can drop in.

Verified mods

Hand-picked by the BOZ Redux team. Mods are downloaded from the Discord, where you can also talk to the people who made them.

How to install a mod

1. Download it

Join the Discord and download the mod's ZIP file.

2. Unzip it

Unzip it into the mods folder of your client.

3. Turn it on

Tick it in the launcher's Mods tab. A map mod is offered when you press PLAY.

What you need

Everything here is free, apart from your own copy of the game.

To play mods

  • A Windows or Linux PC
  • The BOZ Redux client, v0.3.1 or newer
  • Your own Android 1.0.11 APK of the game

To make mods

  • Everything on the left
  • The BOZ Redux SDK (Linux also needs Python 3.11)
  • Blender 5.2 LTS, for maps
