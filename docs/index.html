Introduction

The BOZ Redux SDK is the toolkit for modding Call of Duty: Black Ops Zombies (Android 1.0.11) on PC with the BOZ Redux client. Build your own Zombies maps, make your own weapons, and change how the game plays, all with free tools and no reverse engineering.

No game files come with the SDK. You need your own copy of the game, installed with the client.

What you can do

Build maps. Start from an empty floor, or remix Kino der Toten, Ascension or Call of the Dead. The editor is Blender set up for Zombies: it draws your map the way the game does, and Build & Run puts you in it in one click.

Use everything in the game. Every model, texture, machine, door and trap from every map is in the asset browser, ready to drag in. Perk machines, the mystery box, Pack-a-Punch, wall buys, traps and windows work as soon as you place them.

Bring your own models and textures. Import FBX, glTF and OBJ. The editor makes a game version with the right number of triangles, and shows you how it will look.

Make weapons. Copy any gun, change its damage, fire rate and ammo, or give it your own model. Edit its animations and its Pack-a-Punch upgrade.

Change the sound. Replace any of the game's sounds with your own, give each weapon its own sounds, and play music and voice lines from scripts.

Add effects. Use the game's effects and projectiles anywhere: black holes, explosions, lightning.

Set the rules. Shape every round: how many zombies, their health and speed, how the game scales as rounds climb, with a graph to see it. Set dog rounds, points, power-ups and the box, and give each difficulty and player count its own balance.

Script anything. Group objects into prefabs and give them a Lua script: buttons, traps, teleporters, easter eggs. Extend the game's own objects to change what they do, with settings and links right in the editor.

Change the controls and the screen. Add key binds and pause-menu settings, draw your own windows, or change the field of view.

Play together. Every map works in LAN co-op, and players start it straight from the client's launcher.

Share it. One click packages your work as a mod that players drop into their client.

Where to start

New here? Go through these in order:

  1. Getting started: install the tools and make a map with your first script.
  2. How a map works: the whole model, on one page.
  3. Prefabs: make your own objects, with recipes to copy.
