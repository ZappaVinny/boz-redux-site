Playing on Android

BOZ Redux runs on Android phones and tablets too, with the same mods and maps as on PC. This page takes you from download to your first match. No game files come with BOZ Redux: you need your own copy of the original game.

What you need

  • An Android phone or tablet with Android 8 or newer and a 64-bit processor (almost every phone from the last few years).
  • Your own copy of the original Call of Duty: Black Ops Zombies APK, version 1.0.11.
  • Wi-Fi for the game's data packs (825 MB), or the packs already copied to your phone.

1. Download the client

On your phone, open the BOZ Redux releases and download BOZ-Redux-android-arm64.apk from the newest release.

2. Install it

  1. Open the downloaded file (from the browser's downloads, or your Files app).
  2. Android asks whether the app you opened it from may install apps (Install unknown apps). Allow it for that app, then go back.
  3. Tap Install, then Open.

Android asks because BOZ Redux comes from GitHub instead of the Play Store. You can switch the permission off again afterwards in Settings.

3. Install the game files

BOZ Redux opens on the GAME FILES tab. A box at the top lists what's left to do.

  1. Under 1. Game APK, tap Choose the APK file... and pick your own Black Ops Zombies APK. The status line says Installed: version 1.0.11 when it's done.

  2. Under 2. Data packs, tap Download from Activision (825 MB), and wait for both packs.

    Already have the packs? Tap Import files... instead and choose blackops_etc.dz and blackops_gles1.dz, or the game's .obb file.

When both lines in the box at the top turn green, you're ready.

4. Play

Tap PLAY. Choose MAIN MENU for the game as it shipped, or a map from one of your mods, and SOLO or CO-OP. The game uses its own touch controls. The launcher works upright or sideways; the game always plays sideways.

Mods on your phone

Mods come as .zip files, the same ones PC players use.

  1. Download the mod's .zip to your phone.
  2. In the launcher, open MODS and tap Install a mod (.zip)..., then pick the file.
  3. Its maps appear on the PLAY screen.

To update a mod, install the newer .zip the same way: the launcher shows both versions and asks first. Each mod in the list has Remove... to take it off again. Some mods change their windows to suit a touch screen on their own.

Co-op

Phones and PCs play together. Everyone joins the same Wi-Fi, picks the same map and taps CO-OP. Every player needs the mod that adds the map.

Updates

When a new version of BOZ Redux is out, a line at the top of the launcher says so. Tap Update now: it downloads the new APK and Android asks you to tap Install. Your game files, saves and mods stay where they are. What's new shows what changed, and Later hides the notice until the next start.

If something goes wrong

  • "App not installed" when updating. The copy on your phone came from a different build (a test build, for example). Uninstall it, then install the new one. Uninstalling also removes its game files, so you'll do step 3 again.
  • A data pack won't download. Check your connection and tap Download again, or copy the packs from a PC and use Import files....
  • The game closes or misbehaves. The log is boz-log.txt in Android/data/org.bozredux.client/files/game/. Share it when you ask for help on the Discord.
